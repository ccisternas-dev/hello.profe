# German word card (#68)

Production build with live MyMemory translations from Spanish.

- `desktop-plural-personen-light.png`: "gente" becomes "Personen". The plural alias finds "die Person", a noun with plural "Personen".
- `mobile-das-band-dark.png`: "la cinta" becomes "das Band". The article in the translation limits the card to the neuter entry, out of three genders.
- `desktop-phrase-no-card.png`: phrases ("ein großes Haus") get no card. Words that are not in the lexicon get none either; for example, MyMemory translated "casa" as the compound "Zuhause-Gefühl".
