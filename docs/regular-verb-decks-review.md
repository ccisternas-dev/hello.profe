# Regular verb decks — draft content review

Five local-only decks cover German, English, French, Spanish and Dutch. Each contains 24 cards (six suggested learning items per level from A1 through B2). All new decks have `reviewed: false`; previous approval of the existing 18 decks does not cover these additions.

Each card gives an infinitive, third-person singular forms, a past participle and translations into the other four interface languages. French uses présent, imparfait and passé composé; Spanish uses presente, pretérito indefinido and pretérito perfecto. English uses the regular `learned` variant. Dutch irregular `vragen` and `bezoeken`, Spanish stem-changing `jugar` and irregular `reducir`, and French irregular `réduire` are excluded from their respective regular-verb decks.

These are original AI-drafted selections of useful everyday and work/study verbs, not a statistically ranked list of the most frequent verbs. Level groupings are suggested learning stages, not official CEFR classifications for each word. The 24 cards per language are a starter collection, not comprehensive A1–B2 coverage.

Grammar references consulted:

- [University of Michigan: strong, weak and mixed German verbs](https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/german-resources/grammatik/strong-weak-mixed/)
- [British Council: irregular verbs and regular past forms](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/english-grammar-reference/irregular-verbs)
- [Dutch Grammar: present perfect](https://www.dutchgrammar.com/en/?n=Verbs.re13)

Review in local Flashcards: choose a language, choose Regular verbs, then select Search. Each deck matches A1, A2, B1 and B2 filters; filtering selects the whole deck, not a subset of its cards. Review the forms, translations and suggested levels in `src/data/study-decks.json` before approving publication. The source converter preserves these generated decks.

The original French and Spanish forms follow regular conjugation patterns but have not received independent native-speaker review. All five decks remain drafts until explicit owner approval.
